# Finkfold Student & Parent Portal: The Ultimate User Manual Guide

---

## 🌟 Welcome to Your Student & Parent Portal!

### What is this Portal?
Think of this portal as your **24/7 Digital School Office on your phone or computer**. 

In the past, whenever a parent or student needed something—like checking attendance, paying fees, asking about homework, applying for leave, booking a bus seat, or finding a lost water bottle—you had to write letters in school diaries, make multiple phone calls, or physically travel to the school office and stand in long queues.

**With this portal, you never have to stand in a queue again.** Everything can be done from your phone or laptop in less than 2 minutes.

---

## 🔑 How to Log In (First Step)

1. Open your browser (Google Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox).
2. Go to the website address: **`https://your-school-url.vercel.app/student/login`** (or `http://localhost:3000/student/login` on local testing).
3. You will see a clean, dedicated login screen.
4. You can log in using either:
   - **Your Student ID / Admission Number**: (for example: `PRIY-2026-001` or `001`) with password **`Student@123`**.
   - **Your Student Email**: `student@priyanka.school` with password **`Student@123`**.
   - **Your Registered Parent Phone Number**: (for example: `+919440266743`) with password **`Student@123`**.
5. Click **"Sign In to Student Portal"**.
6. You will immediately land on your personal dashboard!

### 2. 📈 Exams & AI Skill Gaps (`/portal/student/academics`)

#### What is the purpose of this page?
Instead of just giving a single number like *"Math: 65%"*, this page uses Artificial Intelligence to tell you **the exact reason why marks were lost** and gives you customized worksheets to fix those weaknesses over the weekend.

#### What are the 3 tabs on this page?

#### Tab A: "Exam Results & Rankings"
- **What it shows**: Detailed scorecards for Formative Assessments (FA-1, FA-2) and Summative Assessments (SA-1).
- **Key information**:
  - Marks obtained vs. maximum marks (e.g., 88 / 100).
  - Class rank (e.g., **Rank 4 out of 42 students**).
  - Percentile score (e.g., **92nd percentile**).
  - Subject-by-subject comparison against the **Class Average** (so you can see if the exam was difficult for everyone or just your child).
- **Download Official Report Card**: Click the button to download the CBSE-formatted signed PDF report card. (Note: If tuition fees have a pending due, the button will gently remind you to clear dues first).

#### Tab B: "AI Skill & Gap Engine"
- **What it shows**: The AI breaks down every question your child answered in exams and diagnoses specific sub-topics:
  - 🔴 **Critical Gap**: Topics where the student scored below 50% (e.g., *3D Geometry Frustum Volume Formulae*).
  - 🟡 **Developing**: Topics with partial understanding (e.g., *Ray Optics Lens Formula Sign Conventions*).
  - 🟢 **Strong**: Topics where the student has mastered concepts (e.g., *Quadratic Polynomials*).
- **How to use the Remedial Worksheet button**:
  - Click **"Generate 15-Problem Practice Worksheet"** next to any weak topic.
  - The AI immediately generates a targeted worksheet with 15 step-by-step diagnostic questions and answer keys.
  - You can print this worksheet or solve it on paper over the weekend.

#### Tab C: "Earlier Marks Archive"
- **What it shows**: Historical academic archives for Classes 7, 8, and 9.
- **Why it helps**: When applying for higher secondary schools, junior colleges, or scholarships, you often need marks from earlier years. They are permanently stored here so you never lose old papers.

### 6. 💬 Teacher Chat & PTM Scheduler (`/portal/student/ptm-messages`)

#### What is the purpose of this page?
Connects parents with subject teachers respectfully without invading the teacher's personal family time.

#### Tab A: "Regulated Teacher Office Hours Chat"
- **Why is it regulated?** Teachers teach all day and have families in the evening. This feature enforces **Office Hours (03:45 PM – 05:00 PM)**.
- **How to use it**:
  1. Select the teacher you want to speak with from the left list (e.g., *Mrs. K. Radhika - Mathematics*, *Dr. K. Srinivas - Science*).
  2. Check the green badge: If it says *"Office Hours Open"*, the teacher is available and your message will be delivered immediately.
  3. If office hours are closed, you can still type your question. The system will safely **queue** your message on the server and deliver it automatically the next school day at 03:45 PM.
- **Where does it go?** Directly to the teacher's official faculty portal. All messages are archived per child safety regulations.

#### Tab B: "PTM Slot Scheduler"
- **Why does it exist?** During Parent-Teacher Meetings, parents often wait 1 to 2 hours outside a classroom for their turn.
- **How to use it**:
  1. Look at the open 10-minute time slots (e.g., *09:30 AM – 09:40 AM*, *09:40 AM – 09:50 AM*).
  2. Click **"Reserve This 10-Min Slot"** next to your child's teacher.
  3. The slot is locked for you.
  4. On PTM day, you arrive exactly 5 minutes before your time and walk straight into your conference without standing in line!

### 7. 🛡️ Safe Space Drop-Box & Conduct Ledger (`/portal/student/safespace`)

#### What is the purpose of this page?
A 100% confidential and safe space for children and parents to report bullying, harassment, or exam stress without any fear.

#### Tab A: "Anonymous Anti-Bullying Drop-Box"
- **Zero-Identity Guarantee**: When you write a report here, the system **never records** your name, phone number, student ID, or IP address.
- **How to submit a report**:
  1. Select the issue: *Bullying / Intimidation, Online Harassment, Campus Hazard, or Confidential Counseling Chat*.
  2. Select urgency: *Standard*, *High*, or *Critical / Immediate*.
  3. (Optional) Provide the campus location (e.g., *Near the basketball court bleachers*).
  4. Type your message explaining what happened.
  5. Click **"Lodge Confidential Report"**.
- **The Magic of the Tracking Token**:
  - The screen will display a secret code, for example: `SAFE-TOKEN-8819`.
  - **Save this token on your phone.**
  - Later, paste this token into the tracker box on this page to see private advice or responses from the Senior School Counselor—without anyone ever knowing who asked!
- **Where does it go?** Straight to the Principal and Certified School Counselor's emergency queue.

#### Tab B: "Live Conduct Ledger & Merits"
- **What it shows**:
  - **Merit Points (+)**: Points awarded for good behavior, helping peers, sportsmanship, and academic excellence (e.g., *+25 pts for Star Student of the Week*).
  - **Demerit Warnings (-)**: Mild reminders for late entry or missing uniform tie (-5 pts).

### 8. 🎒 Digital Lost & Found Board (`/portal/student/lost-found`)

#### What is the problem it solves?
Children frequently misplace expensive winter blazers, water bottles, sports watches, and geometry boxes. In the past, parents had to rummage through cardboard boxes at the school reception.

#### How to use it:
1. When cleaning staff or teachers find an item, they log it here with an emoji/photo, description, and the exact locker bin where it is stored (e.g., *Bin A-04 Reception Store*).
2. Filter items by category: *Uniforms & Blazers, Water Bottles, Watches, Books & Stationery, Glasses & Accessories*.
3. Search for your child's lost item.
4. When you see your item, click **"Claim This Item"**.
5. Enter your child's name, homeroom, and an **identifying mark** (for example: *"Arjun written in ballpoint inside the collar tag"*).
6. Click **"Submit Claim"**.

#### Where does it go?
The receptionist and homeroom teacher receive an alert. The caretaker verifies your identifying mark and delivers the item directly to your child's homeroom desk the next morning!

### 10. 📅 Digital Leave Management & On-Duty (OD) (`/portal/student/leaves`)

#### What is the purpose of this page?
Apply for student leave online and verify official sports representation credits.

#### Tab A: "Digital Leave Application"
- **How to apply for leave**:
  1. Select the reason: *Sick Leave (1–2 days)*, *Extended Medical Leave (>3 days)*, or *Casual Family Function*.
  2. Pick the **From Date** and **To Date**.
  3. Type the reason.
  4. **Important rule**: If you apply for more than 3 consecutive days of illness, the portal will prompt you to attach a doctor's certificate (e.g., `Dr_Murthy_Certificate.pdf`).
  5. Click **"Submit Digital Leave Application"**.
- **Where does it go?** Directly into the class teacher's attendance ledger. Once approved, the days are marked as authorized leave instead of unexcused absence.

#### Tab B: "On-Duty (OD) Roster"
- **What is On-Duty (OD)?** When your child represents the school at an inter-school Robotics Hackathon, State Swimming Championship, or Science Fair, they cannot attend regular classes.
- **Why it matters**: Their absence is recorded as **"Present - On Duty"**. It gives them 100% attendance credit so their exam eligibility is completely safe.
- **Details shown**: Event name, organizing authority, faculty escort name, and days credited.
